Climate Change is of the biggest issues humanity has ever faced

Climate change is the long-term shift is temperature and weather conditions on Earth. However, in current times, it is more associated with humanity's impact n the environment

Despite being such a big issue, climate change is often overlooked in recent times. Despite the clear effects of climate change in our environment (the stronger storms and heatwaves all over the world), solutions to this crisis aren't covered as much in the news, and education has shifted away from this extremely serious issue.

We are forgetting how important climate change is.

In this website, you will find information on what climate change is, its effects on our environment, and ways that we cna individually help reduce the effects of climate change.
