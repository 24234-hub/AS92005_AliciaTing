Welcome to the Climate Change Advisory!

Climate Change is of the biggest issues humanity has ever faced

Climate change is the long-term shift in temperature and weather conditions on Earth. However, in current times, it is more associated with humanity's impact n the environment

Due to the burning of fossil fuels, which has increase despite efforts to bring down its consumption, we are causing more extreme weather and temperatures globally. This is seen in the stronger storms and heatwaves,, melting ice caps and sheets rising sea levels, and more.

Though this is such a big issue, climate change is often overlooked in recent times. Around the world, we can clearly see effects of climate change in our environment (the stronger storms and heatwaves), yet the solutions to this crisis aren't covered as much in the news. Education has shifted away from this extremely serious issue. People aren't protesting for change. The government no longer focuses on climate change as an issue affecting our lives.

We are forgetting how important climate change is.

In this website, you will find information on climate change. This will include the following.

  • A more in-depth overview of what climate change is
  • What causes climate change
  • What are climate change's effects on our environment
  • Ways we as individuals can help reduce the effects of climate change
